# Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 — Pairing and setup guide

This is an independent reference, not Meta support. Meta’s current documented pairing path uses the Meta AI app and charging case. The case-free procedure from the supplied notes is included below only as an unverified report.

## Official pairing route

1. Update/install the Meta AI app, sign in, enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and make sure the glasses have charge.
2. Slide the glasses’ power switch toward the front of the glasses.
3. Put the glasses in their charging case with the nose bridge connected to the case charging contacts.
4. Wait for the case LED to flash blue. If it does not enter pairing mode, hold the button on the back of the case for at least five seconds.
5. In the Meta AI app, select **Glasses** → **Add** and follow the prompts.
6. Confirm any Bluetooth prompt shown by the phone. Start pairing in the app, not in the phone’s Bluetooth menu.

Meta notes that glasses pair to one Meta account at a time and must be factory reset before another account can pair them. Reset erases photos/videos stored on the glasses; import captures first.

## Supplied case-free field note — not verified by Meta

The attached notes describe folding both arms, holding the capture button for about 12 seconds until an inside LED blinks blue, choosing “No case?” in the app, and confirming the phone prompt. Meta’s current public instructions reviewed for this package do not document this as the standard pairing method. Use it only as an anecdotal workaround at your own discretion; the documented method uses the case.

The supplied notes also recommend closing and reopening the app if setup stalls. Meta’s current troubleshooting article instead starts with charge, app version, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, docking and case pairing mode, and suggests forgetting an existing phone Bluetooth connection before trying again.
