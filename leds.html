LEDs · Know which light

One pair of glasses.
Several tiny lights.

The case LED, the glasses’ notification LED and the camera capture light have different jobs. Don’t mash them into one imaginary color chart.

Quick rule:

For exact notification patterns on your model and software version, use the LED library inside the Meta AI app: Glasses → Learn and explore → Gestures and LEDs → Notification LED.

Charging-case LED

Meta documents these meanings for Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta cases. Context matters: the case reports glasses charge when glasses are docked, and case charge when the case is empty.

Solid green

Docked: glasses fully charged. Case empty: case fully charged.

Solid orange

Docked: glasses charging. Case empty: case is not fully charged.

Blinking orange

Case battery is low when the case is empty.

Blinking red

Charging error. Check the contacts and the official charging guide.

Pairing indicator

For the documented case-based pairing flow, the case LED flashes or pulses blue when the glasses are ready to pair. If it does not enter pairing mode, Meta says to hold the case button for at least five seconds.

Glasses notification & capture LEDs

The glasses’ notification LED signals status changes related to power, capture, voice controls and calls. Meta directs users to the in-app LED library for the meanings; exact color patterns may depend on the glasses model and current software. Do not treat the case-LED colors above as the glasses’ complete LED chart.

The capture indicator is also a privacy signal. Respect it and people around you; don’t cover or disable recording indicators. For camera-specific safety details, use Meta’s official privacy guidance.
