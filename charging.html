Charging · Battery · LEDs

Power facts.
Not cable folklore.

The glasses charge while docked in their matching case. Plug the case into USB-C. The case LED is your tiny traffic light.

Important correction to the supplied notes:

Meta says the glasses charge in the charging case and calls the nose-bridge-to-case connection the only way to charge the glasses. A clip-on USB-C cable at the nose-bridge contacts is not Meta’s supported method; avoid treating seller claims as official charging advice.

How to charge

  1. Place the glasses in their matching charging case, making sure the nose bridge contacts meet the charging port.
  2. Close the case. Its LED blinks to confirm the glasses are charging. If no light appears, clean the glasses’ nose-bridge contacts and the contacts inside the case, then dock them again.
  3. Charge the case using a compliant USB-C cable connected to the port on the bottom of the case and a suitable power adapter.
  4. If the glasses are turned on and connected to the Meta AI app, check their charge status in the Glasses tab.

Gen 2 battery estimate

Up to 8h

Meta lists up to eight hours for a fully charged pair of Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 glasses under moderate use. The fully charged case can provide up to 48 hours of charging for the glasses. Actual use varies.

Quick case LED read

Solid green

Docked glasses: fully charged. Empty case: case fully charged.

Solid orange

Docked glasses: charging. Empty case: case is not fully charged.

Blinking orange

Empty case: low case battery.

Blinking red

Charging error. Check the contacts and refer to official charging support.

LED behavior depends on whether the glasses are docked. The app also lists notification LED patterns for the glasses themselves.
