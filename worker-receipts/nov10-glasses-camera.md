# Camera and capture indicator — replacement source note

This is a newly written source note; the original camera receipt named in the uploaded file tree was not attached.

Meta describes a bright white Capture LED that blinks during photo capture and continues blinking while video is recording. Meta says the camera is disabled if the glasses detect that the Capture LED has been covered, tampered with or destroyed. Meta also recommends respecting people's preferences, showing them how the indicator works, and powering off in sensitive spaces. [Meta AI glasses privacy guidance](https://www.meta.com/ai-glasses/privacy/)

This site does not instruct readers to hide or disable recording indicators. Camera use should be deliberate, lawful and respectful of people nearby.
