# Charging and case LED — replacement source note

This is a new source note for the archive, not the original worker receipt. Meta's case LED descriptions depend on whether the glasses are docked.

- With glasses docked: solid green means the glasses are fully charged; solid orange means they are charging; blinking red means a charging error.
- With the case empty: solid green means the case is fully charged; solid orange means it is not fully charged; blinking orange means the case battery is low; blinking red means a charging error.
- During documented case-based pairing: the case LED flashes blue when pairing mode is ready.

If glasses do not charge, Meta advises checking the nose-bridge/case contacts and docking again. Glasses charge in the case; the case itself charges over USB-C. [Meta charging support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/844064497339059/) [Meta pairing support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/268269592726432/)

Do not apply case LED meanings to the notification LED on the glasses. Meta directs users to the app's LED library for the glasses' detailed notification patterns.
