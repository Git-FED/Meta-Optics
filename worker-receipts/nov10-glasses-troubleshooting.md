# Troubleshooting — replacement source note

This newly authored note is not the missing original worker receipt.

For pairing, Meta recommends: update the Meta AI app; enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi; charge the glasses; power them on and dock them; wait about 45 seconds; then enable case pairing mode and add from the app. If the glasses already show in phone Bluetooth settings, forget that connection and retry from the app. Meta's later steps include reinstalling the app and, if needed, factory resetting the glasses. [Meta pairing troubleshooting](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/852077233025627/)

If the glasses' notification LED blinks white continuously during pairing, Meta suggests a force restart, then retrying setup in the app. A force restart does not delete captures or require pairing again. [Meta restart support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/905490264519056/)

Factory reset is a last step because it deletes photos/videos on the glasses and requires pairing again. [Meta reset support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/814846737027479/)
