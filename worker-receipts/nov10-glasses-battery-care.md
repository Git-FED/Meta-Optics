# Battery care — replacement source note

This newly written note replaces only the filename listed in the attachment; the original receipt was not supplied.

Meta lists Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 at **up to 8 hours** of moderate use on a fully charged pair, with **up to 48 hours of charging** from a fully charged case. These figures vary with use and other factors. They are estimates, not guaranteed continuous playback times. [Meta battery-life support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/303057485648146/)

Meta's supported method is to charge the glasses in their charging case and charge the case with USB-C. The nose bridge must contact the charging port in the case. Meta describes this as the only way to charge the glasses. [Meta charging support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/844064497339059/)

The supplied excerpt's claim about a clip-on USB-C cable at nose-bridge contacts is attributed there to a cable seller, not Meta. This archive does not recommend it.
