# Meta AI app — replacement source note

This newly authored note corresponds to a filename named in the supplied tree; the original app receipt was not attached.

The Meta AI app is the documented place to begin adding AI glasses to a phone. Meta's setup flow instructs users to open **Glasses**, choose **Add**, and follow the prompts. Glasses pair to one Meta account at a time. [Meta pairing support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/268269592726432/)

Meta says pairing preparation includes an up-to-date app, Bluetooth on, Wi-Fi active, charged glasses and a brief wait after placing the powered-on glasses in their case. If a glasses-to-phone Bluetooth connection already exists, forget it and restart pairing from the app. [Meta troubleshooting support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/852077233025627/)

When glasses are turned on and paired, their charge status appears in the Glasses tab. Meta also exposes its detailed notification LED library in the app. [Meta charging support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/844064497339059/) [Meta notification LED support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/757215899749677/)

**Limit:** App labels can change as software updates. Follow the current on-screen instructions.
