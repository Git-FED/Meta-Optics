# Pairing setup — replacement source note

This new note is not the original worker receipt. The standard Meta procedure is: power on the glasses, dock them in their charging case with the nose bridge connected, wait for the case LED to pulse blue, then use the Meta AI app's **Glasses → Add** flow. If the case does not enter pairing mode, hold the case's rear button for at least five seconds. [Meta pairing support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/268269592726432/)

Meta recommends an up-to-date app, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled, sufficient charge, and a short wait after docking. Pair from the app rather than initiating setup from phone Bluetooth settings. [Meta pairing troubleshooting](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/852077233025627/)

The supplied attachment describes a no-case procedure with folded arms, a capture-button hold and an in-app “No case?” prompt. The official pages reviewed for this project do not document that as the standard procedure. The website identifies it only as an unverified personal field note.
