# Privacy and LEDs — replacement source note

This is a newly authored replacement note; the original receipt in the supplied file tree was not included.

There are different indicators. The **notification LED** near the right frame signals power, capture, voice-control and call status. Meta directs users to the Meta AI app's per-device LED library for exact patterns. [Meta notification LED support](https://www.meta.com/help/ai-glasses/757215899749677/)

The **Capture LED** is a recording/privacy indicator. Meta says it blinks briefly during photo capture and continuously during video recording. Meta also says the camera will be disabled if the glasses detect that the capture light was covered or tampered with. [Meta privacy guidance](https://www.meta.com/ai-glasses/privacy/)

Do not obscure recording indicators. Ask before recording, stop if someone objects, and turn the glasses off in sensitive spaces.
