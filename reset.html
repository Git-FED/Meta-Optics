One is a harmless nap. The other wipes your on-glasses photos and videos and asks you to pair again. The orange light is not a casual suggestion.

Factory reset erases content.

Import or back up captures that matter before continuing. A factory reset also returns settings to factory state and requires pairing again.

Factory reset Ray-Ban Meta / Oakley Meta

  1. Remove the glasses from their case. Make sure they are charged and powered on.
  2. Close both arms.
  3. With the arms folded, press the capture button five times. The LED turns orange.
  4. Press and hold the capture button. Release it when the LED flashes.

That is the published reset sequence. The supplied notes describe a short timing window and waiting for a later white/blue/off sequence; those extra details aren’t in the current Meta steps, so don’t rely on them over the official procedure.

Try a force restart first

Meta says a force restart does not delete photos or videos and does not require pairing again.

  1. Slide the power switch back to turn the glasses off.
  2. Press and hold the capture button.
  3. While holding capture, slide the power switch forward to turn the glasses on.
  4. Release capture as soon as the status LED turns red. If it does not turn red, slide power off and repeat.

A regular restart is simpler: switch the glasses off, wait a few seconds, then turn them on again.
