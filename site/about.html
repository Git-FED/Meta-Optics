Clear receipts.

Muse is an independent, unofficial reference site. It is not operated, approved or endorsed by Meta, Ray-Ban or EssilorLuxottica.

What was supplied?

The source attachment contained one quick pairing guide and a file tree naming additional research and “worker-receipts” documents. Those named documents were not attached, so this package does not claim to reproduce or quote their missing contents. The package includes newly written, source-linked notes instead.

How the guide treats claims

Manufacturer instructions are presented as the supported procedure. The supplied notes’ case-free pairing method is labeled as an unverified field anecdote because Meta’s published pairing flow uses the charging case. The clip-on USB-C charging claim is not recommended because Meta says the glasses charge only in their case. Reset advice follows Meta’s current reset steps, with the data-erasure warning placed before the procedure.

Device behavior can vary by generation, model, app version and firmware. Use the LED library inside the Meta AI app for detailed notification patterns on your glasses.
